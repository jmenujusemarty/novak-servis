# Autoservis Janov Logo

Selected direction: Quiet Workshop Wordmark.

The revised direction is much more restrained: a simple car silhouette as a construction line, a strong JANOV wordmark, and a muted service label above it. The logo should feel like a credible local workshop, not a racing decal or clip-art car badge.

Preserve:
- Exact name: Autoservis Janov
- Subtle car silhouette as the automotive cue
- Deep green, charcoal, white, and neutral grey
- Strong readable wordmark with JANOV as the anchor

Avoid:
- Fake certification seals
- Partner or oil-brand marks
- Racing-only styling
- Decorative sparks, crosses, and detailed vehicle illustration

Files:
- `logo-autoservis-janov-new.svg` for white/light backgrounds
- `logo-autoservis-janov-new-dark.svg` for dark backgrounds
- `logo-autoservis-janov-mark.svg` for avatar, favicon, and compact placement

Production caveat: typography is live SVG text and should be converted to outlines before final print production. The concept also still needs trademark and similarity review before official rollout.
